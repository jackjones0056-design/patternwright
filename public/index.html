FAQ

Common questions.

Do we have to replace our existing software?+

Usually no. Patternwright starts by evaluating whether the systems already in place can be connected or configured more effectively.

Does every workflow need AI?+

No. Some problems are better solved with ordinary automation. AI is used when it adds useful judgment, extraction, summarization, drafting, or classification.

Who owns the finished system?+

The client should own the production accounts and subscriptions whenever practical. Patternwright documents the workflow and hands over operating knowledge.

What if the first automation is not worth building?+

Then the Fit Check ends there. The point is to find valuable work, not manufacture an AI project.

Do you build websites, or only automation?+

Both. AI integration and workflows are the primary product. Website builds for local businesses — including redesigns and booking/quote CTAs — are an explicit offering, especially along the Gulf Coast / South Mississippi.
